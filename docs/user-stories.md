# User Stories

1. As a customer, I want to browse the available cookies view their prices and check their stock availability, so that I can choose the cookies I want to purchase. (FR1)
2. As a customer, I want to view customer orders and update their delivery status , so that I can manage and track orders efficiently. (FR2)
3. As a customer, I want to submit reviews and ratings after receiving my cookies, so that I can share my shopping experience and provide feedback to the seller.
